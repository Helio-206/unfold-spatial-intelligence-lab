# Spatial Voice — product acceptance round 2

Base: PR #28 snapshot `fb1f892cdf773a51f61a3f5c745efb2f11fca5d3`. The independent branch does not modify Cloud or main.

The workspace inspector has a question input, a short spoken transcript, state-dependent speech controls, voice preferences and expandable evidence and limitations. The reviewed plan and selected component are used for a bounded universal question contract. The backend `/api/spatial/question` uses the existing origin, rate, concurrency and paid-call budget guards; text is sent only on explicit question submission. Responses are labelled Meta and not verified. On failure, the interface reports unavailability and shows local information. Local explanations distinguish general knowledge from plan evidence. No paid calls were made in validation.

Plan/geometry generation increments a version token; it replaces full-plan serialization on redraw. Changes in component, Space or version abort the question and invalidate speech; late responses are checked against the captured layer, version and selection. The microphone interrupts speech.

Validation: full Vitest 145 files / 2412 tests; build passed. Paid route fixture tests use fake upstream responses; they do not prove a real Meta result. Chromium + SwiftShader at 1440x900 and 390x844 clicked the actual 3D eyepiece and trachea and checked local transcript, play/pause/resume/stop, component change, edit/undo and unsupported speech using a simulated TTS port. A separate simulated Meta test checked a follow-up and a delayed answer after changing selection. Screenshots are in ignored `reports/spatial-voice/evidence/`.

Limits: no live Meta answer or audible browser speech was verified. Native headless Chromium had no voices during the prior check; Safari/iOS real device and screen-reader not available. Browser tests do not yet cover a delayed response on Space change or plan revision, microphone interruption, or resource leak profiling. Keyboard was exercised for a question follow-up; full keyboard accessibility is not established. Model-generated text remains unverified, and general functionality for arbitrary domains depends on the model. Keep draft pending these checks.
