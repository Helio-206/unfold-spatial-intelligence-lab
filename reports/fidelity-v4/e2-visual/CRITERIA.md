# Pre-registered limited study

Frozen before executing the visual reconstruction benchmark. Three independent synthetic reference fixtures are generated using Three.js primitives in the laboratory, never the candidate generator. Each has four fitting and four held-out orthographic masks. The fitter sees only a conceptual plan, selected node, fitting masks and camera calibration. No reference parameters or held-out views enter it. Success for this narrow study requires: at least 0.02 increase in mean fitting IoU; held-out mean IoU must not decline by more than 0.01; and at least two of three fixtures must reduce the absolute error of the selected beam width. All fixtures and failures remain reported. A single-parameter result cannot establish articulated reconstruction, joint inference, or photographic fidelity. Negative tests include absent calibration, insufficient independent views, empty silhouettes, invalid masks, cancellation and exhausted budget. Topology measurements are separate from reconstruction acceptance.
