# Pre-result criteria, synthetic experiment

Predetermined before running the two fixture experiment: report both fitting (four views) and held-out (four different views) mean IoU. A meaningful limited synthetic result requires both joints below 1e-6 plan units after correction, no mutation of input, no orientation or rigid-shape change by the joint solver, and held-out IoU at least the baseline and at least 0.90. These criteria do not establish physical correctness or real-image reconstruction. Shape parameters and local joints are supplied, not estimated from images; the experiment explicitly leaves a 20% beam-width error in place. Failed criteria remain reported rather than tuned after measurement. Also report closure before/after, width error, geometry cost, solver timings (20 independent calls after warmup), and current heap delta as a noisy estimate rather than peak memory. No claim of fitting silhouettes is made: views measure the joint correction only.
