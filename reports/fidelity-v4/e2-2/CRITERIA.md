# E2.2 frozen acceptance criteria

The mission's A–I remain unchanged. For synthetic known-frame fitting, every evaluated candidate must close all supplied hinge anchor pairs to at most 0.005 of the assembly span (0.5%, chosen to make the previous 2.66–5% gaps unacceptable, not an engineering tolerance); the target of the parameterisation is numerical zero. Axis agreement must have absolute dot ≥ 0.999999. A feasible proposal must improve fitting IoU by ≥0.02 and must not be scored as held-out success when held-out IoU decreases. Parameter and view partitions are fixed before the final benchmark. Known frames are disclosed priors, not visually discovered joints. Unknown or ambiguous axes must remain unknown. No visual or numerical threshold establishes a real mechanical joint or physical dimensions.
