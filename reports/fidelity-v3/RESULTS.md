## What was not done, and why

No provider was run (no GPU, no route to the providers, no key, no approved budget, no authorisation to send private images). **There is therefore no evidence here about how SAM 3D Objects, TRELLIS.2, Meshy or Hunyuan3D perform.** Nothing in this PR makes a provider call. The real Meta plans of the lungs and the microscope were not kept in the repository (raw responses are git-ignored), so the comparison inputs are **hand-authored reproductions** of the structure and flaws the Terminal's acceptance report recorded. They are inputs to compare two geometry routes, not proof of generation.

## What was built

- **Router** (`fidelity/router.ts`): starts from the V2 compile (so groups, hierarchy, userData and every failure are V2's), replaces components family by family only where a rule or request says so and limits allow, and records one decision per component (family, reason, fallback code, plan provenance copied, representation, asset id). The plan is never written; the source hash is carried in the report; V2 is always the return path; a scene budget sends the most expensive replacements back to V2.
- **`continuous_surface`**: signed distance fields from the plan's own primitives (10 operator/transform/repeat cases checked against V2 bounds), surface-nets extraction (closed surfaces, topology preserved), junctions to touching neighbours filleted **on one side only** (the thinner part flares into the thicker one). Components are never merged: each keeps its own mesh, group and selection. `extrude`, `revolve` and `loft` have no field here and stay on V2.
- **`mesh_asset`**: a mesh is a *proposal*; nothing is drawn without a recorded approval naming the components; a bad node mapping or a failing limit returns the component to V2. Fitted rigidly to the component bounds.
- **Safe GLB intake** (`fidelity/glb.ts`): container, extensions (Draco refused), embedded-only data, counts, accessor ranges, triangle totals and decoded texture memory checked before any decode; optional declared texture downscale; fuzzed.
- `disposeCompiledSpatialPlan` now frees any material and texture (found by a test: external meshes leaked).

Two defects found by looking at the pixels, not by tests, and fixed: (1) both sides of a junction drew the same fillet and fought into white shards; (2) the reference camera was computed before the workspace knew its usable area. One remains (below).

## Assessment (the assistant's, not a human reviewer's)

**Mechanical, microscope.** The clearest gain of the continuous route. Base, column, arm, revolver, eyepiece and knobs now flow into each other with fillets, and it reads as a cast instrument instead of stacked primitives; the V2 parts only touch. Still the same parts and proportions: no detail the plan lacks (no inclined tube, no curved arm). Flaw: the eyepiece flare is a bulb, too large.
**Organic, lungs.** Mixed. The tracheal ring and the carina now join the trachea, and the bronchi are part of the lobe surface instead of tubes lying on it (V2 leaves a visible gap and a floating ring). But the lobes are still plain ellipsoids (the source has notched, asymmetric lobes), and the carina's fillet onto the lung is a flat pink patch with a **serrated rim** (teeth the size of one grid cell) visible at 2× zoom. Not a recognisable improvement over the source illustration, and not anatomy.
**External mesh.** A different class of result: a real bellows camera and wooden tripod with PBR textures, 20 k triangles, two selectable components. This is what a mesh path can *display*. It says nothing about whether any provider can *produce* such a mesh from an image or text, and it is two components because the artist named two nodes, not because a generator separated parts.

Selection: all components stay pickable in every route (`spatialEntityId` unchanged, layer tests with edit, undo and rebuild through the router). Editing: transforms and undo work through the layer and rebuild through the router, **including its latency (up to 2 s for the lungs)**; a colour edit on a component drawn by an approved mesh would not recolour the asset (not tested).

## Limits that remain

- **Provider quality, licence behaviour, GPU needs, latency and cost are unknown**: see `COMPARISON.md` for the protocol. Hunyuan3D 2.1's licence excludes the EU.
- (Resolved, see `STABILISATION.md`) Continuous surfaces now run in a Worker with a cache; V3 still draws 5–50× more triangles than V2.
- (Largely resolved) The serrated rim is fixed by sub-triangle clipping (≈10× smoother); joints between boxes still leave residual creases.
- Booleans, openings and cuts are **not implemented**: the plan contract cannot express them, and inventing them would put geometry the model never proposed into a reviewed plan.
- Instancing and LOD are limited to V2's instanced repeats and a bounded grid; no LOD chain, no normal-map baking.
- Fixtures are reproductions; they were not tuned to the router, but they are not Meta output and prove nothing about generation.
- Software rendering only; no GPU memory measurement beyond the renderer's own counts.

## GO / NO-GO

- **Technical, router + safe GLB intake + approval contract, as an optional prototype: GO** to review. Plan, source hash and provenance are preserved, nothing replaces a reviewed component without a recorded approval, every failure returns to V2, memory and triangles are bounded, 40 new tests including fuzz and leak checks.
- **Visual, continuous-surface family: partial GO.** Clear for jointed mechanical forms; mixed for organic forms until the rim artefact and the lobe shapes are addressed.
- **Adopting any provider: NO-GO until the protocol in `COMPARISON.md` is run** with a GPU or an approved budget and explicit authorisation per image.
